About Mo' The Picnic Guru

Creating elegant experiences, unforgettable celebrations, and luxury moments filled with beauty and love.

Who We Are

Mo' The Picnic Guru is a luxury picnic and event décor company based in Limpopo, dedicated to creating stylish and unforgettable experiences tailored to every client.


What started as a passion for elegant event styling has grown into a trusted brand known for luxury picnic setups, romantic celebrations, birthday décor, and intimate experiences designed with creativity and attention to detail.


We believe every celebration deserves beauty, comfort, and sophistication — allowing our clients to enjoy meaningful moments while we bring their vision to life.

Our Story

Mo' The Picnic Guru began with the vision of turning ordinary gatherings into luxury experiences through elegant picnic styling and creative décor.


As demand for beautifully curated celebrations grew, the business expanded into birthday celebrations, romantic setups, kiddies parties, and customized event styling.


Today, the brand continues to grow through professionalism, creativity, and a passion for creating memorable moments that clients cherish forever.
